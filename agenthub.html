01 / Context

The question behind the work.

An education kit needs a clear first interaction. The web demo gives the student an entry point, while the product render helps explain the physical kit. I helped organize those materials around the intended learning experience.

My contribution covered product framing, demo validation, documentation and visual material organization. The work involved checking how the introduction, interface and product explanation fit together.
