01 / Context

The question behind the work.

When several projects share the same week, finding the right entry point becomes part of the work. I designed this page to bring the daily task module, weekly pulse and project launcher into one view.

My work covered the homepage structure, navigation and layout. The interface explores how much context should be visible before opening an individual project.
