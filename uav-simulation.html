01 / Context

The question behind the work.

A planner becomes easier to study when its inputs and outputs are visible. I built and reviewed simulation workflows around UAV autonomy, formation behavior, obstacle avoidance and path planning. Linux, ROS configuration and visualization are part of that work.

My research starts from FAST Lab’s open-source EGO-Planner implementation as a baseline. I use it to establish a reference for subsequent integration and evaluation. The research questions concern coordination and the information a swarm needs to share.

The working material includes simulation screenshots and planning study artifacts. I use them to connect a visible behavior with its configuration and underlying software path. That discipline also informs the interactive swarm on this website.

03 / Next iteration

Where the work stands.

Research in progress

The next stage is a controlled evaluation of coordination methods against the baseline, with repeatable scenarios and explicit constraints. The browser hero illustrates control and formation concepts; its lightweight motion logic has a separate implementation from the ROS planner.
