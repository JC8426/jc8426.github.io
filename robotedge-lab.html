01 / Context

The question behind the work.

A lab project brings together research questions, hardware decisions and practical scheduling. I helped organize this context through notes, research maps and follow-up tasks, so the next discussion could begin with a shared record.

My role covered coordination and documentation support: capturing the technical question, identifying what needed clarification and preparing material for review. Technical decisions remained part of the team’s discussion.

02 / Practice

How I approached it.

01

Capture

Write down the question, the current understanding and the points that remain open.

02

Organize

Connect research ideas with tasks and supporting material.

03

Follow up

Bring the records back into the next discussion and update the next steps.

This work developed my ability to move between a technical discussion and a readable project record. The same skill was useful during RCAP, where interfaces, software behavior and observations from the field had to be understood together.
